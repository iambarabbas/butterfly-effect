The partnership

The Butterfly Effect partnered with the American Cancer Society and the Mall of America to conduct 5,000 Butterfly Drops at Race for the Cure events worldwide. Each person who found a butterfly artistically decorated the front and wrote their personal cancer story on the back. These heartfelt stories transformed the butterflies into powerful symbols of hope and resilience.

The installation

The decorated butterflies were collected and assembled by Tasha and her team into a stunning large-scale installation displayed at the Mall of America. As people walked through, they were deeply moved by the shared experiences of survivors, caregivers, and those affected by cancer — a vibrant testament to the strength and unity of the community.
