The idea

The softest flutter of a wing can set off a chain reaction that produces major change.

Artist and entrepreneur Tasha Wahl fused Gandhi's timeless call to “be the change” with Edward Lorenz's Butterfly Effect theory — and built a worldwide movement of contagious generosity through interactive art.

Since 2013, thousands of Butterfly Drops and hundreds of Social Butterflies have raised nearly $320,000 for more than 500 charitable organizations.

Two ways to flutter

Give unexpectedly
Butterfly Drops

Random acts of kindness, left in the world for strangers to find — the movement's original spark.

Give together
Social Butterflies

Interactive public art installations that fan waves of social-media giving for causes worldwide.
