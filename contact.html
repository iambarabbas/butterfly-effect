Meet the team

The people behind the flutter

Tasha Wahl
Founder · Idea Creator

I love getting lost in a good book. I love running through the canyon in the rain, with my dogs, Chloe and Zoe, splashing in the puddles beside me. I love butterflies—their symbolism of hope and transformation inspires me. I love dancing in the pit at concerts, feeling the music take over. I love learning, exploring, and traveling. I love being surrounded by my three boys and their ever-growing circle of friends. I love that I truly love my work. I love that I get to share both life and business with my soulmate of 30 years. And I love the magic of dropping butterflies—watching the ripple effect, seeing who they touch, and knowing that together, we’re making a difference.
