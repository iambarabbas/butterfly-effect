A member of our butterfly tribe

Founder Tasha Wahl catches up with longtime butterfly ambassador Nicole Bianchi, whose "purple butterfly" story was a formative early moment for the movement and who has since led a project installing 12 Social Butterflies around her community. Nicole shares a story of helping three young women take a photo at her town's butterfly, discovering that each of them had a deeply personal connection to butterflies tied to grief and loss.
