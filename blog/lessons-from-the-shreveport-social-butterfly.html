Lessons from the Shreveport social butterfly

A Shreveport, Louisiana organizer recounts how their Social Butterfly installation — painted on a building in the Allendale neighborhood — became the most successful of all 200 installations worldwide, drawing about 550 participants and raising over $10,800 for local nonprofits in a single month. The piece breaks down five reasons it worked: a community hungry for engagement, a 30-day fundraising challenge that created urgency, strong nonprofit partnerships, proactive local media outreach, and enlisting local social media influencers to spread the word.
