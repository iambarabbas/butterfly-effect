The idea

The softest flutter of a wing can set off a chain reaction that produces major change.

Artist and entrepreneur Tasha Wahl fused Gandhi's timeless call to “be the change” with Edward Lorenz's Butterfly Effect theory, and built a worldwide movement of contagious generosity through interactive art.
